# Warning Label for Humans: One Line, Big Laugh — 要求与候选

## 要求（源自官网 description，2026-11-25 核对）
- 一行喜剧：应该印在每个人身上的安全警告标签，至多 160 字符。
- 好笑第一，可信第二。
- 评审维度：先过切题关，再比笑点、共鸣感、标签措辞、160 字符内契合度。

## 候选
1. WARNING: Requires 8 hours of charging nightly; will run on 4 and make it everyone's problem. Keep away from group chats after midnight. ✅ 选定（135 字符）
2. WARNING: Contents under pressure. May leak at weddings, funerals, and dog food commercials. Do not shake during Mondays.
3. CAUTION: May spontaneously start a diet on Monday and abandon it by Thursday. No user-serviceable parts inside.
4. WARNING: Side effects include recalling every lyric from 1998 while forgetting why it entered this room.
5. WARNING: Handle with snacks. Becomes emotionally unavailable when hungry and intellectually unavailable before coffee.
6. WARNING: This unit makes major decisions based on vibes and one article it skimmed. Not approved for assembling furniture or arguments.
7. CAUTION: Prone to saying "I'm fine" at 40% battery. Actual state may vary. Consult snacks before continued use.
8. WARNING: Do not expose to deadlines, group projects, or the phrase "per my last email." Sudden sighing may occur.
9. WARNING: Factory settings include overthinking. Updates arrive at 3 a.m. and cannot be postponed.

## 选定理由
候选 1：完全按产品标签语法写（Requires… / will… / Keep away from…），"充电 8 小时、只充 4 小时就让所有人买单"是人人有共鸣的真实现场，最后一句把场景钉死在深夜群聊。候选 8 的 "per my last email" 也很好，但偏职场小众；候选 1 普适性更强。节奏：三段标签短句，干脆利落。135 字符合规。
